## Derived artefacts committed in this repository

These are derived in part from the CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 Kaggle data. Their licence is therefore
**CC BY-NC-SA 4.0** (attribution as above):

- `data/processed/*` (for example `calibration.json` and `fidelity_report.json`);
- `web/public/data/*` (the precomputed dashboard artefacts).

Synthetic data is generated by this project. It describes a **fictional** operator and must never be
presented as real company data.
