## 1. Decision summary

No single public dataset covers everything (requirements R1–R6), so we use **two real datasets plus a near-real synthetic UAE layer**.

| # | Dataset | Host | Role | Download? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **FreshRetailNet-50K** | Hugging Face | Real stockouts in fresh retail: the evidence for the core problem, and the real-world test of our demand-recovery and forecasting method | **Yes (required)**, ~115 MB |
| 2 | **Supermarket Sales Data (vegetables)** | **Kaggle** | Real prices, wholesale costs, spoilage rates and 3 years of continuous sales, for unit economics, waste and seasonality | **Yes (required)**, ~54 MB |
| 3 | FreshRetailNet-LT | Hugging Face | A second real stockout dataset spanning Jun 2023–Jul 2025, used as a robustness check | Optional, ~317 MB |
| n/a | **UAE synthetic layer** (stores, SKUs, festivals, spikes, suppliers, stock ledger) | Generated by our own script in Phase 5 | Runs the dashboard scenarios, with known true demand | Nothing to download |

Real inputs that scripts fetch in Phase 5, so nothing needs downloading by hand:
- UAE weather (Open-Meteo);
- transport emission factors (UK DESNZ 2025);
- shelf-life guidance (USDA FoodKeeper);
- food emission factors (Poore and Nemecek, 2018);
- the official UAE festival calendar.

## 3. The two required datasets

### 3.1 FreshRetailNet-50K: real hidden stockouts

- **Link:** https://huggingface.co/datasets/Dingdong-Inc/FreshRetailNet-50K
- **Files:** `data/train.parquet` (106.4 MB) and `data/eval.parquet` (8.4 MB).
- **Release details:** created 7 May 2025, last updated 9 Oct 2026, licence CC BY 4.0.
- **Paper:** Wang et al. (2025), arXiv 2505.16319.
- **Why we need it.**
  - It is the only public perishable dataset that records *when shelves were empty* (hourly stock status). About 20% of its data is affected by stockouts.
  - The paper shows that forecasts trained on its raw sales under-predict demand by 7.37%.
  - With it we can measure the hidden-demand bias on real data and test our recovery method against real stockouts.
  - It also lets us calibrate:
    - intraday demand shapes;
    - stockout timing (under 2% after the 6 am restock, rising to 26% by 8 pm);
    - promotion effects;
    - weather effects.
- **Limits:**
  - The data is from China.
  - Sales are scaled by an undisclosed coefficient.
  - There are no prices, costs, suppliers or inventory quantities.
  - It covers only 90 days.

### 3.2 Supermarket Sales Data (vegetables): real economics and spoilage

- **Link:** https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/yapwh1208/supermarket-sales-data
- **Files:** 4 CSVs totalling 53.7 MB:
  - `annex1.csv`: items and categories;
  - `annex2.csv`: transactions from 1 Jul 2020 to 30 Jun 2023, with date, time, item, kg sold, selling price (RMB/kg), sale or return, and a discount flag;
  - `annex3.csv`: daily wholesale price;
  - `annex4.csv`: average loss rate per item, ranging from 0% to 29%.
- **Licence and standing:** CC BY-NC-SA 4.0. Kaggle usability score 10.0, with about 36.6k downloads.
- **Provenance:** the Kaggle page only says it is vegetable sales from a supermarket. Its structure and period match the data released with the 2023 China Undergraduate Mathematical Contest in Modeling (CUMCM), Problem C, on vegetable pricing and replenishment (as used in Ma, 2024). We will cite it as the Kaggle dataset and note this apparent origin.
- **Why we need it.**
  - It is real perishable data with **costs and spoilage**, which FreshRetailNet lacks.
  - Retail versus wholesale price gives real margins. Margins drive the order-quantity maths, because the cost of being short is weighed against the cost of waste.
  - The loss rates calibrate waste.
  - Three continuous years show seasonality and festival lift shapes (ramp-up, peak, drop-off).
  - The time stamps give an intraday profile.
- **Limits:**
  - It covers one store only.
  - There is no stock or stockout data.
  - The data is from China and is vegetables only.

## 4. What the real data cannot cover, and how we generate it

The synthetic UAE layer is generated by a seeded, versioned script in Phase 5. It covers **1 Jan 2023 – 31 Oct 2026** at daily granularity, plus hourly detail for a recent window. That span includes four Ramadans (2023–2026) and the 2026 disruption. It allows a clean **backtest**: train the models on 2023–2025, then test on Ramadan 2026 combined with stockpiling and a supply shock.

| Gap | Why real data can't cover it | How we generate it (near-real) | Calibrated from | Label |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **UAE operator, stores, SKUs, prices in AED** | All real data is Chinese, and FreshRetailNet has no prices | A fictional composite operator with about 8 dark stores (Dubai, Sharjah, Abu Dhabi) and about 40 fresh SKUs in about 8 categories: leafy greens, berries, stone fruit, salad vegetables, dairy, bakery, poultry and dates. AED costs and prices are documented assumptions, and markups follow the real wholesale-to-retail ratios. | Kaggle annex2/annex3 | Calibrated synthetic, fictional company |
| **Multi-year UAE seasonality and festivals (R3)** | No public Gulf grocery data exists | Daily true demand = base × weekly pattern × annual seasonality × festival lift × weather × promotion × noise. Festival dates are the real ones: Ramadan, Eid al-Fitr, Eid al-Adha, National Day, New Year, school breaks. Lift *shapes* come from real festival behaviour. Ramadan *size* is anchored to published retail data. | Kaggle 3-year history, FreshRetailNet holiday effects, NielsenIQ (Ramadan ≈ 19% of annual MENA FMCG sales), Worldpanel (Mar 2026 peak) | Calibrated synthetic |
| **True demand versus recorded sales (the core problem)** | Real data never shows true demand during a stockout | Generate true demand, then run a baseline "order what sold" policy. Recorded sales = min(demand, stock on shelf), and stockout hours are logged. **The ground truth is therefore known.** | FreshRetailNet stockout share and intraday stockout curve | Calibrated synthetic (ground truth) |
| **Stock-in / stock-out ledger, on-hand stock, waste (R1)** | No public dataset has these for perishables | An inventory simulation with stock ageing (first in, first out). Receipts by supplier are the stock-in. Sales plus expiry waste are the stock-out. On-hand stock is tracked per day and per hour. | Shelf life from USDA FoodKeeper; spoilage levels from Kaggle loss rates | Simulated |
| **UAE weather** | FreshRetailNet weather is Chinese | Real daily weather for Dubai, Sharjah and Abu Dhabi | Effect sizes from FreshRetailNet | **Real** weather, calibrated effects |
| **Promotions** | The promotions in the real data are Chinese | A synthetic promotion calendar. Uplift is calibrated, including the real finding that deeper discounts raise stockouts. | FreshRetailNet discount data, Kaggle discount flag | Calibrated synthetic |
| **Sudden spikes, e.g. 200 → 500 (R4)** | Real spikes are rare and unlabelled | A spike engine covering two cases. *Announced* events: a festival, a promotion, a big match. *Unannounced* shocks: stockpiling, a heatwave, a viral item. Size, duration and warning time are dashboard sliders. Defaults are 2.5×, plus a "March 2026 stockpiling" preset. | Peak-to-average ratios measured in the real data; Worldpanel (2026) | Scenario |
| **Suppliers and procurement criteria (KQ5)** | No public UAE fresh-supplier data | 6–8 suppliers in 4 archetypes: UAE local vertical farm, GCC overland, sea import, air import. Criteria: landed cost, lead-time mean and variability, on-time %, freshness on arrival, quality rejects, chokepoint exposure, CO2e per kg, minimum order. | Lead-time stretch (Gulf Business, 2026); route exposure (Atlantic Council, 2024); transport emission factors (DESNZ, 2025) | Synthetic suppliers with real emission factors |
| **Supply disruption (Hormuz-type)** | No public data on UAE fresh lead times | A scenario in which sea-route lead times stretch and become more variable, reliability drops and freight costs rise. Includes a Mar–May 2026 preset. | Gulf Business (2026); *Sustainability* (2026); optionally IMF PortWatch chokepoint transits (real; usage terms to check) | Scenario informed by public reporting |
| **Sustainability metrics (KQ4)** | No dataset holds waste CO2e or food-miles | Waste in kg is converted to CO2e with per-category food factors. Transport CO2e = mode factor × route distance. | Poore and Nemecek (2018); DESNZ (2025) | Derived: real factors × synthetic volumes |
| **Real-time feed (KQ3)** | We can't run live operations | An accelerated replay of the synthetic hourly stream | n/a | Simulated live feed |

## 5. Keeping the synthetic data "near-real"

1. **Fidelity checks.** We compare the synthetic data with the real datasets on:
   - intraday demand shape;
   - variability per SKU;
   - share of zero-sale days;
   - stockout share and timing;
   - festival peak-to-average ratio;
   - discount uplift;
   - spoilage distribution.
   
   Tolerances are set in Phase 3. The results go in a report appendix.
2. **Ground truth.** Because we generate true demand, we can measure exactly how much demand stockouts hid, how biased a naive forecast is, and how much the AI recovers. **Real data can never show this.** Every such number is labelled synthetic.
3. **Reproducibility.** A fixed random seed, a versioned generator, and a metadata file recording sources and parameters.
4. **No fabrication.** Every assumption is listed with its reason and source. Synthetic results are never presented as a real company's results.

## 6. Labelling rules

| Tag | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **REAL** | Unmodified real data, with its source cited | FreshRetailNet stockout curve; UAE weather |
| **CALIBRATED SYNTHETIC** | Generated by us, with parameters taken from real sources | UAE store demand, prices, ledger |
| **SCENARIO** | A user-driven what-if | 200 → 500 spike, Hormuz preset |
| **SIMULATED FEED** | A replay presented as "live" | Real-time stock tracker |
| **DERIVED** | Real factors × synthetic volumes | Waste CO2e |
| **PRECOMPUTED** | Computed offline and served as static files | Model forecasts, backtests |

- **Files.** Synthetic outputs live in `data/synthetic/`, carry a `_synthetic` suffix and come with a metadata JSON.
- **Dashboard.** Every chart carries its tag and source. A persistent notice says the operator is fictional and that data is calibrated synthetic unless marked REAL.
- **Report.** A data-provenance table, a provenance statement in every figure caption, and the duplication of KQ2 flagged.

## 9. Flags

1. Neither real dataset is from the UAE. The UAE layer is synthetic by design and labelled everywhere.
2. FreshRetailNet sales are scaled by an undisclosed coefficient, so real-data results are reported as **percentages** (for example, bias in %), not as unit volumes.
3. The provenance of the Kaggle vegetable data is inferred, not stated on the page. It will be cited as "appears to be CUMCM 2023 Problem C data".
4. Ramadan and Eid dates for 2023–2026 will be compiled from official UAE announcements and cited in the report.
